## About Me

I'm **ShenGeYan**, a **Web Frontend Developer** and a **Computer Science & Technology** student at **Chongqing University of Technology**.

I enjoy building interfaces that are clear, smooth, and genuinely useful. My work usually sits around frontend engineering, product interaction, AI-assisted development, and small tools that make the creation process easier.

- Building frontend apps with **Vue 3**, **React**, and **TypeScript**.
- Exploring **SSR**, **SEO**, resource platforms, collaborative tools, and bot-driven workflows.
- Turning experiments into maintainable projects with a clean structure and practical details.
